Navin Sriram

Last updated: October 2025

I am a final year undergraduate student at IIT Madras. My research interests lie in using reinforcement learning and more recently, multimodal representations to improve decision making in complex systems, from robotics to trading.

I was fortunate to spend the summer of '24 and '25 as a RISS Scholar at the Robotics Institute, CMU. I am currently collaborating with Andrew Jong and Jay Patrikar under Prof. Sebastian Scherer, working on achieving resilient planning in the wild with multimodal reasoning. I have previously worked with Yufei Wang under the Guidance of Prof. David Held and Prof. Zackory Erickson on robot assisted dressing with arm motions using deep RL and multimodal finetuning. At my home university, I work under the Guidance of Prof. Asokan Thondiyath and Prof. Bijo Sebastian on a wide variety of interesting topics revolving around ML and Robotics. I have been a part of Raftar Formula Racing, buidling electric race cars.

I have always loved learning difficult things and building cool stuff, which has been my motto so far. I have worked broadly on topics ranging from Offline RL, active perception for field robots, Robot Learning, Computer Vision for ADAS, Multimodal ML and Safe Control.

Publications
Don’t Fool Me Twice: Adapting to Failures in the Wild with Multi-Modal Reasoning
Navin Sriram, Andrew Jong, Kayla McFarlane, Jay Patrikar, Bijo Sebastian, Sebastian Scherer
In submission, Prelim. work accepted at IROS 2025 "Surviving Failures in the wild : The Art of Robustness" Workshop

Grounding anomalous behaviour with semantic scene level features and modelling the behaviour in volumetric space with voxel-level Gaussian processes to learn from mistakes and adapt accordingly. Any robot, anywhere.

ClutterNav: Gradient-Guided Search for Efficient 3D Clutter Removal
Navin Sriram, Keerthi Vasan M, Bijo Sebastian
In submission at a top conference

Improving decision making for sequential clutter removal to retrive objects of interest while trying to minimize structural disturbances and total number of removals

Multi-Modal Fine-Tuning for Robot-Assisted Dressing with Arm Motions
Alexis Hao, Yufei Wang, Navin Sriram, Bharath Hegde, David Held, Zackory Erickson
Accepted at Confernce on Robot Learning (CoRL) '25, Seoul

Non-collaborative single arm robot assisted dressing where we adapt the policy to work with occluded pointcloud observation and fine-tune with force to help the policy adapt to moving arm motions

Real-world offline reinforcement learning from VLM feedback
Sreyas Venkataraman*, Yufei Wang*, Ziyu Wang, Navin Sriram, David Held, Zackory Erickson
Accepted at IEEE & RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) '25, Hangzhou

Learning reward functions using VLM preferences from task observation from an offline dataset for complex tasks

QuickGrasp: Lightweight Antipodal Grasp Planning with Point Clouds
Navin Sriram*, Keerthi Vasan M*, Bijo Sebastian, Asokan Thondiyath
Accepted at IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) '25, Atlanta

Analysing the grasp planning at the contact point space instead of an eef sampling problem in 6d. leveraging constrained optimization to rank candidates using force closure and slippage based constraints

Projects

Here are some cool projects I worked on

Model based Offline RL with Bidirectional Imagination
Fall 2024, Prof. Balaraman Ravindran, DA7400 : Recent Advances in RL

Improving offline model-based RL with bidirectional imagination for dataset augmentation, employing an ensemble BNN for modelling forward and reverse dynamic models with generative reverse rollout policy reducing distributional shift and forward policy improvement based on an adversarial 2 player game. Integration of ideas from ROMI and RAMBO-RL

Image Restoration with Defect Preservation using DL
Fall 2024, Prof. AN Rajagopalan, EE5179 : Deep Learning for Imaging

Image denoising while preserving defective regions using a tiny dataset with degraded, clean and defect mask triplets. Nice project to implement and play around with SOTA architectures and techniques to understand their merits/demerits. Employed a fusion encoder type architecture distilling features from popular SOTA backbones like UNet and ResNet. Also employed Image translation techniques, using Cycle GANs for learning the noise patterns and expanding the dataset

Hybrid decoupled planning for Multi-Agent Path Finding
Spring 2024, Prof. Bijo Sebastian, EE5179 : Intro. to Motion Planning

End-to-end planning for MAPF with decoupled local planners operating within a dynamic local window.Global Path Planning using RRT* and Trajectory tracking using pure-pursuit on a set of three Turtlebot3 robots. Collision avoidance using D* Lite to avoid obstacles and Reciprocal Velocity Obstacles to avoid agent-agent collision

Formula Student EV Powertrain testbench Modelling and Testing
Raftar Formula Racing

Developed a mathematical model for the Motor Controller for operation through Field Oriented Control. Performed extensive testing and diagnosis of a high power 3 phase Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motor. Constructed peripherals for safe operation, including precharge, discharge, and HV monitoring and isolation circuits. Also developed a Liquid based cooling system for the drivetrain resulting in lowered temperatures
